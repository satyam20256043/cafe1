# Zordical Owner Guide

*For café, restaurant and hotel owners and managers. About 15 minutes to read. Keep it open on your phone while you set up.*

Zordical answers your customers on WhatsApp, takes table orders through QR codes, and brings customers back with loyalty points and offers. You stay in charge: anything unusual comes to you, and you decide how much discount the AI may give.

## Your first 30 minutes

When you signed up, you got three things. Keep them somewhere safe:

- **Your login link**, which looks like `zordical.com/login/your-cafe-id`
- **Your username**
- **A temporary password**, shown only once

Your café ID is the last part of your login link. It's also shown at the top of your dashboard. Lost your details before saving them? Message Zordical support (see [Getting help](#getting-help)).

Work through the steps below in order. The **🚀 Get Set Up** card on your dashboard ticks them off as you go.

### Step 1: Sign in and change your password

Open your login link and sign in. Then go to **Settings → My Account** and choose a new password of at least 8 characters that you don't use anywhere else.

> Tip: on your phone, open the dashboard and use your browser's **Add to Home Screen**, so it opens like an app. Tap the 🔔 bell at the top and allow notifications to hear about new orders and messages.

### Step 2: Add your menu

Go to **Menu & Pricing**.

1. Tap **+ Add Item** for each dish or drink and fill in the name, category and price.
2. **Disc %** is a regular discount you already give on that item. Customers see the lower price with the old price next to it.
3. When something runs out, tap its **Available** label. It switches to **86'd** (sold out), so customers can't order it.
4. Tap **Save Menu**. Your QR menu and your AI's answers update straight away.

Further down, **Category Order** sets the order of the category tabs your customers see.

### Step 3: Fill in your café details

Go to **Settings → Branch Details** and fill in everything you can: timings, contact number, Wi-Fi name and password, Google Maps link, Google Review link, and your Zomato, Swiggy and Instagram links. Tap **Save Details**.

Your AI answers customers from these details, so the more you fill in, the fewer questions come to you. Don't skip the **Google Review link**: without it, the AI can't send happy customers to review you on Google.

In **Settings → Café Theme** you can choose the look of your customer website.

### Step 4: Connect WhatsApp

This is what lets your AI chat with customers. Use the phone that has your café's WhatsApp number.

1. Go to **Settings → WhatsApp Connection** and tap **📱 Connect by QR**.
2. On the café phone, open WhatsApp → **Linked devices** → **Link a device**.
3. Scan the QR code on your screen.

When it shows **Linked to** your number, you're live. The WhatsApp dot at the top of the dashboard shows whether you're connected. Keep that phone charged and online.

Best setup: link the café's own WhatsApp number here, and keep your personal mobile as the owner number on your account (the number you signed up with). Your alerts, reset codes and reports go to that owner number.

**Quick connect (QR) or Business API?** Quick connect is the fast way and suits most cafés. It handles conversations: replies to customers, your alerts and order messages. Messages sent to many customers at once (broadcasts, campaigns, Auto-Pilot and win-back offers) need the official **WhatsApp Business API** connection instead. Setting that up with Meta takes a few days. Message Zordical support and we'll do it with you.

### Step 5: Teach your AI the things only you know

In **Settings → 📚 Teach your AI**, answer the common questions: parking, veg or non-veg, pets, payment methods and so on. Add more with **+ Add your own question**, then tap **Save Café Facts**.

Prefer to do it by chat? Tap **📱 Train over WhatsApp** and answer the questions your AI sends to your WhatsApp.

The AI never guesses. If a customer asks something you haven't covered, it tells them it will check, and forwards the question to you.

### Step 6: Print your table QR codes

Go to **Table QR Codes**, enter how many tables you have, and tap **🖨 Print All**. Stick one code on each table. Each code knows its table number, so orders arrive marked with the right table.

### Step 7: Test it like a customer

- Open **Chat Simulator** and type the questions customers ask, in English, Hindi or Hinglish. Nothing you type here reaches a real customer.
- Scan one of your QR codes with your phone and place a small test order. Watch it appear in **Orders & Revenue**.
- From a different phone, send your café's WhatsApp a message such as "What time do you open?"

### Step 8: Set your discount rules

Customers will ask for discounts. In **Menu & Pricing → AI Discount Limit**, set the most the AI may give on its own, then tap **Save Limit**.

- **0%** (the default): every discount request comes to you in **Offer Requests**.
- **Above 0%**: the AI gives up to that much straight away, with a coupon code the customer shows at billing. Bigger requests still come to you.

The **AI Max %** column in your menu sets a different limit for one item. For example: 10% across the café, but up to 25% on Cold Coffee.

Just below, **⏰ Follow-up offer** sets the discount for customers who go quiet in a chat, or switches it off. See [Follow-ups](#follow-ups-for-customers-who-go-quiet).

## Your daily 5-minute routine

**Morning**

1. Open **Overview** and deal with the **⚠️ Needs You** card first. These are customers waiting on you.
2. Check **Reservations** for today's bookings.
3. Check **Offer Requests** and approve or reject them.

**During service**

- Keep **Orders & Revenue** or the **Kitchen Display** open and move orders along.

**Evening**

- Skim **Customer Chats** to see what people asked.
- Add the day's bills in **Expenses**.

**Every Monday**

- Read your growth suggestion and weekly report. See [Your numbers and your data](#your-numbers-and-your-data).

On a phone, the bar at the bottom has Overview, Orders, Reservations and Menu. Everything else is under **More**.

## How your AI receptionist works

### What it handles on its own

Your AI replies on WhatsApp and on your café website, in English, Hindi or Hinglish to match the customer. It answers questions about your menu, prices, timings, location and Wi-Fi, plus the facts you added in Teach your AI. It also takes table bookings, shares your Zomato and Swiggy links when people ask about delivery, and handles discount requests within your limit.

It only uses what you've given it: your menu, your settings and your café facts. Change those and its answers change straight away.

### When it hands over to you

The AI stops and brings you in when:

- a customer complains or asks for a refund
- a customer raises a payment problem
- someone wants to book for a group of 8 or more
- it's asked something your information doesn't cover

The customer gets a polite reply saying the team will get back to them. You get a WhatsApp alert with their message and a suggested next step, and the conversation appears in the **⚠️ Needs You** card on Overview.

Reply from **Customer Chats**, or tap **💬 WhatsApp Customer**. When you've sorted it out, tap **✓ Mark Handled**. For questions the AI couldn't answer, add the answer in **Teach your AI** so it can handle them next time.

### Discount requests

If a request is within your AI Discount Limit, the AI gives the customer a coupon code straight away. If it isn't, the AI says it's checking with the manager, and the request appears in **Offer Requests**. **Approve** lets you write the offer, which is sent to the customer on WhatsApp with a coupon code. **Reject** sends them a polite no.

### Follow-ups for customers who go quiet

If a customer stops replying partway through a chat, the AI follows up once:

1. After **10 minutes**: a friendly check-in, with no discount.
2. **15 minutes** later, if there's still no reply: an offer for that day only, with a coupon code.

You choose the offer in **Menu & Pricing → ⏰ Follow-up offer**. The box shows the % being used now (10% unless you've changed it). Set it to **0** to switch the offer off: quiet customers then get only the friendly check-in. Follow-ups stop while your plan is paused.

### Replying yourself

**Customer Chats** shows every conversation, newest first. Search by name, phone number or words. Open a chat and type in the box at the bottom to send a reply straight to the customer's WhatsApp.

### Daily AI allowance on the trial and Starter

During the free trial and on the Starter plan, the AI writes up to **30 replies a day**. After that, open questions get no automatic answer until the next day. Those messages still arrive in Customer Chats, so you can reply yourself. Growth and Pro have no daily limit.

## Orders, tables and the kitchen

### What your guests see

A guest scans the QR code on their table, browses your menu, adds items and places the order with their name. A phone number is optional for dine-in. They then see the order's progress live as you update it.

The bill adds GST at 5%. Guests pay at the counter, or online if you've added your Razorpay keys in **Settings → 💳 Razorpay Payments**. Online payments go straight to your own Razorpay account.

### Handling orders

**Orders & Revenue** shows the live order queue. Move each order along with its buttons (confirmed, preparing, ready, served) or cancel it. Use **+ New Order** for phone or counter orders, and the filter at the top to see orders by status.

When you mark an order **Served** (or **Delivered**), the customer earns loyalty points and a stamp, and gets a WhatsApp thank-you showing their card. This needs their phone number on the order.

The tab also shows how many orders you had today, this week and this month, your **🏆 Top Sellers**, and a chart of daily revenue for the last 14 days.

### Kitchen Display

Open **Kitchen Display** on a tablet or screen in the kitchen. It's in the sidebar, and in the banner at the top of **Table QR Codes**. New orders appear as tickets straight away. Sign in on that device with your username and password. Want a separate login for your kitchen staff? Message Zordical support.

### Delivery

To take delivery orders directly, without Zomato or Swiggy commission:

1. In **Settings → 🛵 Delivery**, tick **Offer delivery ordering**.
2. Set your delivery fee, the order value above which delivery is free, any minimum order, and a note about where you deliver.
3. Optional: tick **Require my approval before the kitchen sees a delivery order**. New delivery orders then wait in **🕐 Awaiting Approval** on Orders & Revenue until you accept them.
4. Tap **Save Delivery Settings**.
5. Copy your **Delivery Ordering Link** from the **Table QR Codes** tab and share it on Instagram, WhatsApp or your website.

Delivery customers must give a phone number and address. They get WhatsApp updates as their order moves along.

### Reservations

The AI takes bookings in the chat (name, number of guests, date and time). Every booking appears in **Reservations** so you can plan the day. **Approve** sends the customer a WhatsApp confirmation. **Cancel** tells them you can't take the booking and invites them to pick another time. Groups of 8 or more come to you in **Needs You** first.

After each of these, the dashboard tells you whether the WhatsApp message reached the customer. If it couldn't (for example, WhatsApp isn't connected), contact them yourself.

## Bringing customers back

### Loyalty points and stamps

Every completed order earns points and one stamp. 10 stamps earn a free item. Customers move up loyalty tiers as they keep coming back.

Set the rules in **Settings → 🎁 Loyalty & Discounts**:

- **Points earned per ₹1 spent** and **points needed for ₹1 off**. The screen shows what this works out to in rupees.
- A **minimum** number of points before a customer can redeem.
- Whether points **expire** after some months without a visit. Turning this on never removes points already earned.
- An optional **student discount**.

The **Loyalty & Rewards** tab shows your members, a points leaderboard and recent activity. Use **Manual Points Adjustment** to add goodwill points or fix a mistake (enter a minus number to deduct). **Upcoming Birthdays** lists customers with a birthday in the next 7 days. Tap **Wish** next to a customer to send them a birthday message on WhatsApp with a free-dessert code. **Send WhatsApp Wishes** sends to everyone on the list at once; that needs the WhatsApp Business API, like other campaigns.

### Reviews and ratings

Customers can rate you in the WhatsApp chat. After a **5-star** rating, the AI invites them to review you on Google. Lower ratings stay private: they appear in **Reviews → In-app feedback** so you can follow up.

**Google review claims.** After posting on Google, the customer tells the AI the name they reviewed under. A claim then appears in **Loyalty & Rewards → ⭐ Google Review Claims**. Check your Google listing for a review under that name, then:

- **Approve**: the customer gets 100 loyalty points and a 15%-off coupon, and a WhatsApp message telling them so.
- **Reject**: if you can't find the review. The customer gets a WhatsApp message asking them to check it.

Check this list every few days so customers aren't kept waiting.

Connecting your **Google Business Profile** (AI-drafted replies to Google reviews, Google posts and listing insights) is coming soon. Until then, the Google card in Settings says it isn't available yet.

### Your customer list (CRM)

**CRM** keeps a profile for every customer who chats with you, orders or is added by you: their visits, tier, tags (such as "coffee lover") and rating.

- **🎯 At-Risk Customers**: customers who haven't been back in 14 days or more, with suggested offers to win them back.
- **🆕 New Customers**: customers who joined in the last 7 days.
- **🚶 Register Walk-in**: add a customer who visited without ordering through Zordical.
- **🔍 View Insights**: type a phone number to see one customer's full history.

### Campaigns and Auto-Pilot

These send messages to many customers at once, so they need the **WhatsApp Business API** connection (see [Step 4](#step-4-connect-whatsapp)).

- **CRM → Broadcast Campaign**: choose a group (all customers, coffee lovers, offer seekers and so on), write a message and send it.
- **AI Campaigns → ✨ Generate AI Suggestions**: the AI suggests an offer for each group of customers. Nothing is sent until you approve it.
- **Auto-Pilot**: switch it on at the top of the dashboard to send day-of-the-week offers automatically, such as a Tuesday special. Set the offers in **Settings → Auto-Pilot Campaigns**.

## Your numbers and your data

- **Overview** shows your reservations, customer count, average rating and pending offers, plus a **When You're Busy** chart comparing today with your 7-day average.
- **📈 Growth Suggestion**: on Mondays, when there's something worth trying (a slow weekday, regulars drifting away, birthdays coming up), a suggestion appears on Overview and on your WhatsApp.
- **📊 Weekly report**: on the Pro plan and during your free trial, a growth and revenue report arrives on your WhatsApp every Monday morning. It's also shown on Overview.
- **Expenses**: log your bills by category (ingredients, salaries, rent and so on) with the vendor, date, GST paid and receipt number.
- **Data Sheets**: your complete records of orders, customers, menu items, expenses and reservations. Search them, filter by date, and tap **Export CSV** to download. Your data is yours.

### Owner Portal

Want a simpler view? Open `zordical.com/portal/your-cafe-id` and sign in with the same username and password. It shows today's orders and revenue, your plan, your chat history and a quick menu editor, with links to the full dashboard. It's also where you renew your plan.

## Your plan and payments

You start with a **30-day free trial**. Plans and prices are on [zordical.com](https://zordical.com).

**Renewing:** open your Owner Portal, tap **💳 Subscription**, pick a plan and pay online. Or message Zordical support. We'll remind you on WhatsApp 5 days and 1 day before your trial ends, and 3 days before a paid plan ends.

**If your plan ends**, your AI keeps working for 3 more days. After that, these pause until you renew:

- automatic AI replies
- follow-ups, campaigns and Auto-Pilot
- growth suggestions and the weekly report

These keep working the whole time: QR ordering, your menu, your café website, your dashboard and all your data. Customer messages still arrive in Customer Chats, so you can reply yourself. When you renew, everything switches back on.

## Passwords and logins

- Change your password any time in **Settings → My Account**, or with **🔑 Change Password** in the Owner Portal. It must be at least 8 characters.
- **Forgot your password?** On the login page, tap **Forgot your password?** and enter your username. We'll send a 6-digit code on WhatsApp to the phone number on your account. The code works for 10 minutes. After 5 wrong tries it stops working, and you'll need to request a new one.
- The code is sent through your café's WhatsApp, so this only works once WhatsApp is connected. If it isn't, message Zordical support and we'll reset your password.
- For your safety, you're signed out after about 8 hours. Just sign in again from your login link.
- Need another login for a manager or the kitchen? Message Zordical support.
- Never share your password with anyone, including people who say they're from Zordical. We will never ask for it.

## When something isn't working

**The AI isn't replying to customers**

1. Check the WhatsApp dot at the top of the dashboard. If you're not connected, go to **Settings → WhatsApp Connection** and link again.
2. Make sure the café phone is switched on and has internet.
3. Look for a plan banner at the top of the dashboard. If your plan has ended, AI replies are paused.
4. During the trial or on Starter, you may have used today's 30 AI replies.

**The AI gave a wrong answer**

Fix the source and the AI uses the new information straight away: **Menu & Pricing** for dishes and prices, **Settings → Branch Details** for timings and contact details, **Teach your AI** for everything else.

**A dish shows the wrong price on the QR menu**

Edit it in **Menu & Pricing** and tap **Save Menu**.

**I'm not getting alerts**

WhatsApp alerts go to the owner number on your account and are sent through your café's WhatsApp, so it must be connected. For dashboard alerts, tap the 🔔 bell and allow notifications. To change your owner number, message Zordical support.

**Campaigns or broadcasts aren't sending**

These need the WhatsApp Business API connection, not Quick connect (QR). They also pause while your plan is paused. After a broadcast, the CRM tab shows how many customers it actually reached.
