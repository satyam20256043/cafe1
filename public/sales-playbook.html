Zordical — Pitch Playbook

The strategic approach behind every pitch — phone, in-person, or video. For the exact words to say on a phone call, see Sales Call Guide; this doc is the philosophy and reusable reference that script is built on.

The core mistake to avoid: explaining the product. Café owners care about their business, not your features. Every step below exists to keep the conversation about them, not about Zordical.

The flow

1. Introduction

"Hello sir/ma'am, I'm Satyam, founder of Zordical. We've built an AI platform specifically for cafés that helps increase repeat customers, automate customer communication, and grow revenue. I just wanted 5 minutes to show you something that might genuinely help your business."

2. Ask questions — get them talking

  • How do customers usually contact you? WhatsApp or calls?
  • Do you collect customer details after every visit?
  • How do you bring customers back after their first visit?
  • Do you run any loyalty or reward program?
  • How do you promote offers?
  • Do you know how many customers haven't visited in the last month?

Their answers become your whole pitch — you're about to repeat their own words back to them.

3. Create the problem

"Many cafés spend a lot on getting new customers, but most don't have a system to bring those customers back. Losing existing customers is often more expensive than acquiring new ones."

4. Introduce Zordical

"That's exactly why we built Zordical. It's an AI Growth Platform that helps cafés get more repeat customers, automate WhatsApp conversations, manage customer relationships, run marketing campaigns, and understand their business through one dashboard."

Don't list every feature.

5. Talk benefits, not features

See the reframing table below — this is the single most important habit in the whole pitch.

6. Show the demo

"Let me quickly show you our presentation."

Share zordical.com/pitch and walk through it together.

7. Show the future

"We're continuously improving the platform. Early partner cafés also get to influence which features we build next."

Makes them feel like a partner, not a customer number 4,000.

8. Close

"Would you be interested in trying it in your café? We'd love to work closely with you and understand your requirements."

Feature → benefit reframing

Never say the left column out loud. Always say the right column.

Never sayAlways say
"Our platform has AI." / "AI Chatbot""Customers receive instant replies even when your staff is busy — even outside business hours."
"We have CRM." / "Customer Database""You'll know which customers visit regularly and which ones haven't returned — instead of losing that information after every visit."
"Marketing Automation""You can automatically send offers to customers who haven't visited recently — the right offer to the right customer, not the same message to everyone."
"We have Analytics." / "Executive Dashboard""You'll know exactly what's increasing your revenue and what needs attention — no spreadsheets to maintain."

Talking points (use to back up the reframes above)

  • Customer retention — existing customers are your biggest asset.
  • More repeat visits — a customer who comes back every week is worth more than constantly finding new ones.
  • AI WhatsApp receptionist — instant responses, even outside business hours.
  • Customer database — finally know who your customers are.
  • Loyalty — give customers a reason to come back.
  • Marketing — target the right customers with the right offers, not a blast to everyone.
  • Executive dashboard — business performance in one place, no spreadsheets.
  • Time saving — staff focus on serving customers; routine communication runs itself.

The one-line pitch

If they ask "So what exactly do you do?":

"Zordical helps cafés get more repeat customers, automate customer communication, and grow revenue through AI — all from one platform."
